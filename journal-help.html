Journal table

Journal title
The journal's name in this release. More info opens its identifiers, publisher, field breakdown and metrics by year.
OpenAlex field
The most frequent primary-topic broad field among the journal's classified articles and reviews in the preceding five years. The percentage beside it is that field's share of classified publications. These assignments use Raw publications for both metric treatments. Unknown means there are no classified publications; older runs may use source-level fields.
Publications
Articles and reviews published in the five years before the selected citation year. Filtered counts those with a recorded OpenAlex reference; Raw also includes publications without recorded references.
Citations
Citations in the selected citation year to those publications, excluding journal self-citations. Counts follow the selected Raw/Filtered treatment and describe incoming citations in the full source graph used for the export, rather than only those from the selected journal universe.
Pubs. w/out refs.
The percentage of eligible articles and reviews without a recorded OpenAlex reference: 100 × (Raw publications − Filtered publications) ÷ Raw publications. It describes missing references in the five-year publication window and complements reference coverage. Missing recorded references can reflect incomplete OpenAlex data as well as publications with no references. This percentage is the same for Raw and Filtered metrics. A dash indicates an unavailable percentage, including when there are no eligible publications.
NF — Network Factor
The journal's share of citation-network prestige in the selected universe. Citations are weighted by the prestige of the citing journal. NF sums to 100 across that calculation universe; larger journals can receive a larger share.
ANS — Article Network Score
NF divided by the journal's share of eligible publications in the selected universe. Its publication-weighted mean is 1; an ANS of 2 represents twice that mean. Missing ANS does not imply missing NF.
Percentiles · ANS / Percentiles · NF
The shared heading names the ranking indicator used for both percentile columns. Field percentiles are shown initially, with ANS selected. VU Amsterdam (SBE) shows Total percentiles only. Advanced settings can show either, both or neither. Higher values mean higher ranks; 100 is the highest percentile.
Field
The journal's percentile among eligible journals assigned to the same OpenAlex field, after applying the universe, treatment, publications-without-references and publication-history requirements.
Total
The journal's percentile among all journals retained after the top-percentage cutoff within each field. A journal outside that cutoff may have a Field percentile but no Total percentile. See Field and Total percentiles for the calculation and tie rules.
Publications in … · Cited in …
The group heading identifies the five publication years and the selected citation year. For citation year 2026, the publication window is 2021–2025.
OpenAlex snapshot … · Live metrics / Frozen metrics
The snapshot month identifies the OpenAlex data used for this year's metrics. Live metrics may be updated in later releases; frozen metrics stay tied to their published data version.
OpenAlex / Norwegian Register
The group heading above NF and ANS names the single journal universe used to calculate those metrics. The same journal can have different metrics in different universes.

A dash means that a value is unavailable; a numeric zero is a defined value. Use × beside a column header to hide it in the main table. Journal title always remains visible. Restore columns brings back hidden columns. Hiding columns also removes them from selection downloads. It keeps the preset, sort order, filters, percentile calculation and journal popup unchanged. These display choices last for this visit; Reset or reloading the page restores the columns.

Search and presets

Search by journal title, publisher, ISSN, or OpenAlex ID. The search and results table use the same visible settings. Publisher and field names can also match a search. An empty search shows the entire current selection.

Citation year
Defaults to the latest full calendar year available in the data. A newer, incomplete citation year can be selected manually. Publications are from the preceding five years. Live metrics may change with later snapshots; the table shows the OpenAlex snapshot month.
OpenAlex fields
All fields is checked initially, with individual fields unchecked: there is no field restriction. Selecting an individual field unchecks All fields; you can select more than one. Checking All fields clears the individual selections. Clearing the last individual selection also returns to All fields. The field shown beside a journal is its most frequent primary field among classified articles and reviews in the five-year publication window; the percentage is that field's share. The full breakdown is under more info. Unclassified journals remain visible without a field restriction and form their own percentile group.
Journal universe
OpenAlex provides the broadest available selection in this release. Norwegian Register restricts the table and metric calculation universe to journals belonging to that register in the selected year. Membership alone does not guarantee that every metric is available.
Max coverage (noisy)
The initial preset uses the latest available full citation year, OpenAlex and Filtered metrics. It shows Field percentiles only, ranked by ANS, with less than 80% of publications without references, output in at least four of five years, and the top 100% per field. Journals without percentiles remain visible, including journals with NF but missing ANS.
Close to EF and AIS
The same Filtered ANS percentile requirements as Max coverage (noisy), using the Norwegian Register universe and Field percentiles only. The name describes the choice of universe and treatment; it does not imply numerical equality with EF or AIS.
VU Amsterdam (SBE)
Uses the Norwegian Register universe, Filtered ANS, less than 80% of publications without references, output in at least four of five years, and the top 75% per OpenAlex field. It shows Total percentiles only and hides journals without a Total percentile.
Custom and Advanced settings
Custom opens Advanced settings. The +/− Advanced settings link also opens or closes the panel. Choosing any named preset closes it. The panel contains open-access and reference-treatment choices, followed by the shaded percentile controls. Changing the universe, treatment or percentile requirements selects Custom unless the configuration matches a named preset. Search, citation year, field and open-access filters do not change the preset. Reset clears display choices while keeping the citation year.
Open access journals only
Restricts the displayed selection to journals marked open access in the OpenAlex source metadata. This describes the journal, rather than the access status of each individual publication.
Include publications without references (not recommended)
Unchecked by default: Filtered metrics include articles and reviews with at least one recorded OpenAlex reference. Checking this option uses Raw metrics, which also include publications without recorded references. Missing references can reflect incomplete data as well as reference-free publications. The choice changes the underlying metrics and percentile calculation. See the methodology and working paper for background.

With a restricted search and no matches, “Search all OpenAlex journals” selects Max coverage (noisy) and clears field and open-access restrictions, retaining the search text and citation year. Filtered metrics remain selected.

Percentile settings and result counts

Show percentiles
Choose Field percentiles, Total percentiles, or both. None clears both; clearing the last selection returns to None. This selection applies to the main table, journal history and selection downloads. Manually hiding a main-table column with × does not change it; Restore columns restores only columns allowed by the current settings.
Ranking indicator
ANS or NF determines the metric being ranked for the selected percentile types. The shared table heading identifies that metric.
Share of publications w/out references
A strict maximum for the percentage without recorded OpenAlex references. Below 80% excludes exactly 80% and higher; it is equivalent to reference coverage strictly above 20%. No maximum also allows journals with an unavailable percentage.
Years with publications
The number of preceding publication years containing eligible Raw output. At least 4 years includes four or five years; No minimum imposes no history requirement.
Retain top % per field
The cutoff applied after Field percentiles are calculated. It determines the pool used for Total percentiles. Field percentiles remain available outside that retained pool.
Hide journals without percentiles
With Field only, require a Field percentile. With Total selected, including both, require a Total percentile. None disables this filter. Manually hiding a column with × keeps the filter active.

A defined value for the ranking indicator is always required. Both broad presets use Filtered ANS, less than 80% of publications without references, at least four publication years and top 100%. VU Amsterdam (SBE) retains the top 75% and hides journals without a Total percentile.

The result count is your current selection out of all exported journals in the chosen universe. Journals with percentile counts journals with a Field percentile when Field alone is selected, or with a Total percentile when Total is selected. It covers the whole selected universe and stays unchanged by search and display filters.

Download this selection offers CSV or XLSX. Both save every matching journal across all result pages, in the displayed order, using only the visible columns and their table headers. The OpenAlex field name and its percentage are exported as separate columns; hiding that field column removes both. NF and ANS use those names, and percentile headers identify their metric. Journal details and settings metadata are omitted. Percentages are numeric on a 0 to 100 scale. XLSX preserves numeric values, missing cells and literal text in an Excel workbook. Complete data and code links to full annual files and previous releases.

Metrics by year

Citing year
The year in which citations are counted. Publications come from the preceding five years: for example, citing year 2026 uses publications from 2021–2025. The smaller text identifies whether that year's data is frozen or live and the OpenAlex snapshot month. Frozen data stays tied to its published run; live data can change with later releases.
Journal universe
The journal's memberships in that year: OpenAlex, or both OpenAlex and the Norwegian Register. Membership can change between years. NF and ANS use the single universe named in the table title, even when the journal belongs to both.
Level
The Norwegian Register level for that citing year. A dash means no level is recorded. Multiple levels, such as “1 | 2”, preserve different levels attached to register entries associated with the same scored journal identity.
Publications
The number of eligible articles and reviews published in the preceding five years. Raw includes all eligible articles and reviews; Filtered includes only those with at least one recorded reference. Zero is an observed count.
Citations
Citations in the citing year to those publications, excluding journal self-citations. Counts follow the selected Raw/Filtered treatment. They describe incoming citations in the full source graph used for the export; they are not restricted to citations from the selected journal universe.
NF — Network Factor
The journal's share of citation-network prestige in the selected universe. Citations are weighted by the prestige of the citing journal. NF sums to 100 across the calculation universe. Larger journals can receive a larger share.
ANS — Article Network Score
NF divided by the journal's share of eligible publications in the selected universe. Its publication-weighted mean is 1; a value of 2 represents twice that mean. Raw/Filtered and universe choices can change both NF and ANS.

A dash indicates an unavailable value or a journal outside the selected score universe. It is different from a numeric zero. A row marked “Not in the data for this year” has no exported journal record for that year.

Field and Total percentiles

Use Show percentiles in Advanced settings to choose Field, Total, both or None. This choice applies to the main table, journal popup and selection downloads. The main table uses Percentiles and the compact journal popup uses Pctiles; the shared heading identifies ANS or NF. Manually hiding a main-table column does not change the percentile selection for other tables. Higher is higher-ranked; 100 is the highest percentile.

Field
The journal's percentile among eligible journals assigned to the same OpenAlex field in that year, after applying the selected universe, Raw/Filtered, publications-without-references and publication-history requirements. Each journal is grouped by its predominant field, not split between the fields in its publication breakdown.
Total
The journal's percentile among all journals retained after the chosen top-percentage cutoff within each field. This is the final combined comparison pool. A journal outside that cutoff can have a Field percentile but no Total percentile.

Every year uses its own journal population and field assignments, with your current ranking settings applied consistently to all years. These are recalculated comparisons, not saved settings from earlier visits. Search, field filters and other display filters do not redefine the ranking pool.

Percentiles are the percentage of comparison journals with a score less than or equal to the journal's score. Ties receive the same highest shared rank. The top-percentage cutoff retains field percentiles strictly above 100 minus the selected percentage, keeping ties together. Journals failing the eligibility requirements show dashes in both percentile columns.

Fields covered

OpenAlex primary fields of … publications
The header identifies the preceding five publication years whose articles and reviews define the field breakdown. The field names below are publication classifications; Other fields combines all named fields beyond the top three.
100% / Unavailable
100% is the total share of classified publications, divided among the rows below. Unclassified publications are excluded. Unavailable means that the breakdown cannot be shown, including when there are no classified publications.
Norwegian Register field (…)
The field assigned to the journal in the selected citation year. Assigned field describes a journal-level classification, without a publication percentage. Multiple assignments appear beneath the shaded header, with the relevant register ID on each row.

OpenAlex: the three most frequent primary-topic broad fields of the journal's articles and reviews from the preceding five publication years. Each classified publication contributes to one field. Other fields combines the remaining named fields. Percentages use classified Raw publications, even when the metrics table shows Filtered scores. Unclassified publications are excluded; percentages are rounded together to total 100%. With no classified publications, shares are unavailable.

Norwegian Register: the field assigned to the journal in the selected year. “Assigned field” describes a journal classification, rather than a publication percentage. If associated register entries have different fields, each entry's field appears on a separate row with its register ID.

Some scored journal identities correspond to several register entries. The main journal-page link uses a unique title match when available; other register IDs stay accessible as links. When no unique main entry can be selected, the IDs are displayed equally. Choosing a display link does not change the journal's scores or merge any additional records.

Published years and source snapshots

These headers appear in the data table on the About page.

Score year
The citation year represented by that row, using publications from the preceding five years.
Status
Frozen means the year's data stays tied to its published version. Live means it can follow a newer release and may change.
Data run
The identifier of the archived calculation release providing that year's data. Its link opens the release files. The run identifier is distinct from the citation year and snapshot month.
OpenAlex snapshot
The month of the OpenAlex source snapshot used for that year's metrics.
Norwegian Register snapshot
The date of the register data used to determine journal memberships, fields and levels. Annual membership and level still refer to the relevant citation year.

Other journal details

The journal popup has a section bar beneath its title: Details, Fields covered and Metrics by year, followed by a close button (×). The bar stays visible while you scroll. Each link jumps to the section's heading, leaving the title visible below the bar. Metrics by year takes you directly to the historical table below the fields. The close button remains available in the bar.

Open access journal
The journal's open-access status in the OpenAlex source metadata. Unknown means no status is available.
Publication years
How many of the preceding five publication years contain eligible output in the Raw data.
Share of publications w/out references
The percentage of eligible articles and reviews without a recorded OpenAlex reference, using all Raw articles and reviews in the five-year publication window. It is separate from OpenAlex field classification coverage.
