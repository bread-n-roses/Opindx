Search

Citation year and status
Calendar year, whose journal citations (to publications from the preceding five years) enter the score. A live year follows the newest OpenAlex snapshot and can still change. A frozen year always comes from the same, shown OpenAlex snapshot and no longer changes, so it can be cited safely. Twelve months after the end of the score year its status changes from live to frozen. For example, 2025 is updated live with every OpenAlex snapshot until the end of 2026. At that point its status and metrics are frozen.
OpenAlex fields
The most frequent primary-topic broad field among the journal's articles and reviews in the preceding five years. Unclassified journals remain visible and form their own group (unknown).
Journal universe
OpenAlex includes all journals in the OpenAlex database with at least one available journal network score, offering the broadest coverage. Norwegian Register is restricted to journals classified as Level 1 or 2 in the selected year, with coverage roughly comparable to the SSCI, SCI, AHCI, and ESCI journal lists. Membership alone does not guarantee that every metric is available.
Preset · Max coverage (noisy)
The default preset uses the latest available full citation year, all fields, and the OpenAlex journal universe. It offers the broadest journal coverage for the table and score calculation, though its metrics can be somewhat noisier. Open Advanced settings to see how its percentiles are defined.
Preset · Closest to EF and AIS
This preset uses the latest available full citation year, all fields, and the Norwegian Register journal universe. It uses the setting that produces results closest to the official Eigenfactor® and Article Influence® Score metrics. It does not imply numerical equality with EF or AIS. Journals without percentiles are hidden by default. Open Advanced settings to see how its percentiles are defined.
Preset · VU Amsterdam (SBE)
Uses the preset Closest to EF and AIS but with different percentile settings.
Preset · Custom
Custom opens Advanced settings for own adjustment. The +/− Advanced settings link also opens or closes the panel. Reset clears display choices while keeping the citation year.

Advanced settings

Show percentiles
Field percentiles are percentiles within the OpenAlex field in the table selection. Total percentiles compare eligible journals retained after applying the percentile settings in the selected universe.
Ranking indicator
The metric used to rank journals: ANS or NF. Journals need an available value for this metric to receive a percentile.
Share of publications w/out references
The maximum percentage of publications without recorded OpenAlex references. Below 80% excludes journals with 80% or more missing references. No maximum also includes journals whose percentage is unavailable.
Years with publications
The minimum number of years with articles or reviews in the preceding five years, including publications without references. At least 4 years requires publications in four or five of those years.
Retain top % per field
The share of highest-ranked journals retained from each field for Total percentiles. Journals outside this selection can still have a Field percentile. Equal scores receive the same percentile and stay together at the selection cutoff.
Hide journals without percentiles
Shows only journals with a Field percentile when Field alone is selected, or a Total percentile when Total is selected. None turns this filter off.
Open access journals only
Restricts the displayed selection to journals marked open access in the OpenAlex source metadata. This describes the journal, rather than the access status of each individual publication.
Include publications without references (not recommended)
Filtered metrics include articles and reviews with at least one reference recorded in OpenAlex. Checking this option uses raw metrics, which also include publications without recorded references. This is not recommended because missing references may reflect incomplete data or publications that genuinely have no references, adding noise to the metrics and percentile calculations. See the research behind it for more details.

Journal table

Table header
Shows the five publication years and the selected citation year. The snapshot month identifies the OpenAlex data used for this year's metrics. Live metrics may be updated in later releases. Frozen metrics do not change anymore and are tied to the shown OpenAlex snapshot version.
OpenAlex field
The most frequent primary-topic broad field among the journal's articles and reviews in the preceding five years. The percentage is that field's share of all articles and reviews published by that journal in those years. Both metrics use non-filtered publications for their calculations. Unknown means there are no classified publications.
Publications
The number of articles and reviews published in the five years before the selected citation year.
Citations
The number of citations in the selected citation year. Citations are to the publications shown in the table. Journal self-citations are excluded. Counts include citations from all OpenAlex journals even when they are not in the selected universe. NF and ANS, however, use only citations between journals in the selected universe.
Pubs. w/out refs.
The percentage of publications (articles and reviews) without a recorded OpenAlex reference in the five-year publication window. Missing recorded references can reflect incomplete OpenAlex data as well as publications with no references in reality. A dash indicates an unavailable percentage, including when there are no eligible publications. The percentage is: 100 × (Raw publications − Filtered publications) ÷ Raw publications.
NF · Network Factor
The journal's share of citation-network prestige in the selected universe. Citations are weighted by the prestige of the citing journal. NF sums to 100 across that calculation universe; larger journals can receive a larger share. The Network Factor follows an Eigenfactor®-like methodology.
ANS · Article Network Score
NF divided by the journal's share of eligible publications in the selected universe. Its publication-weighted mean is 1; an ANS of 2 represents twice that mean. Missing ANS does not imply missing NF. The Article Network Score follows an Article Influence® Score-like methodology.
Percentiles · Field
The journal's percentile among eligible journals assigned to the same OpenAlex field (after applying the percentile settings). See Advanced Settings for the calculation and tie rules.
Percentiles · Total
The journal's percentile among all journals retained after applying the percentile settings. See Advanced Settings for the calculation and tie rules.
General
A dash means that a value is unavailable; a numeric zero is a defined value. Use × beside a column header to hide it in the main table. Journal title always remains visible. Restore columns brings back hidden columns. Hiding columns also removes them from selection downloads. It keeps the preset, sort order, filters, percentile calculation and journal popup unchanged. These display choices last for this visit. Reset or reloading the page restores the columns.

Details

OpenAlex details
A link to the journal's dedicated OpenAlex page, with its OpenAlex data and ID.
Norwegian Register details
A link to the journal's dedicated Norwegian Register page, with its register data and ID.
Open access journal
Whether the journal is recorded as open access in the OpenAlex database.
Publication years
The number of years in the five years preceding the citation year in which at least one publication is recorded for the journal.
Share of publications w/out references
The percentage of publications (articles and reviews) without a recorded OpenAlex reference in the five-year publication window. Missing recorded references can reflect incomplete OpenAlex data as well as publications with no references in reality. A dash indicates an unavailable percentage, including when there are no eligible publications. The percentage is: 100 × (Raw publications − Filtered publications) ÷ Raw publications.

Fields covered

OpenAlex primary fields
The three most frequent primary-topic broad fields among the journal's articles and reviews in the shown five years, with their publication shares. Other fields combines the remaining fields.
Norwegian Register field
The journal's assigned field in the selected citation year. The Norwegian Register assigns one field to each journal, reviewed annually. This is a journal classification, not a publication percentage.

Metrics by year

Citing year
The year in which citations are counted, using publications from the preceding five years. The smaller text shows live or frozen status and the OpenAlex snapshot month.
Journal universe
The journal's membership that year: OA means OpenAlex; OA · NR means both OpenAlex and the Norwegian Register. Scores use the universe named in the table title.
Level
The journal's Norwegian Register level that year. Multiple levels indicate linked register entries with different levels.
Publications
The number of articles and reviews published in the preceding five years. Filtered counts only those with at least one recorded reference; raw includes those without references too.
Citations
Citations in the citing year to those publications, excluding journal self-citations. Counts follow the raw/filtered setting and include citations from all OpenAlex journals. NF and ANS, however, use only citations between journals in the selected universe.
NF · Network Factor
The journal's share of citation-network prestige in the selected universe. Citations are weighted by the prestige of the citing journal. NF sums to 100 across that calculation universe; larger journals can receive a larger share. The Network Factor follows an Eigenfactor®-like methodology.
ANS · Article Network Score
NF divided by the journal's share of eligible publications in the selected universe. Its publication-weighted mean is 1; an ANS of 2 represents twice that mean. Missing ANS does not imply missing NF. The Article Network Score follows an Article Influence® Score-like methodology.
Pctiles · Field / Total
The journal's percentiles for that year, using the metric named in the heading. See Advanced settings.

Data versions

Score year
The citation year, using publications from the preceding five years.
Status
A live year follows the newest OpenAlex snapshot and can still change. A frozen year always comes from the same, shown OpenAlex snapshot and no longer changes, so it can be cited safely. Twelve months after the end of the score year its status changes from live to frozen. For example, 2025 is updated live with every OpenAlex snapshot until the end of 2026. At that point its status and metrics are frozen.
Data run
The archived release supplying that year's data. The link opens its files.
OpenAlex snapshot
The month of the official OpenAlex data snapshot used to calculate the metrics.
Norwegian Register snapshot
The date of the official Norwegian Register data used. Journal membership, fields and levels refer to the selected citation year.
