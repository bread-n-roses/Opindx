Citation year
The year in which citations are counted. OJF uses the preceding two publication years; ANS uses five. Live years can change with a newer snapshot. A year freezes twelve months after it ends: 2025 stays live through 31 December 2026.
Journal metric · Article Network Score (ANS)

ANS measures influence per article. Citations from influential journals carry more weight, following the citation-network method of Bergstrom, West and Wiseman (2008). Journal self-citations are left out.

It uses citations in the selected year to articles and reviews from the five preceding years. Its publication-weighted average is 1; an ANS of 2 means twice that average. The Article Network Score follows an Article Influence™ Score-like methodology.

ANS values are independent calculations, not official Article Influence Scores. Comparisons are most useful within the same field and year.

See this study for a 2023–2024 comparison between ANS and AIS.

Reference
Bergstrom, C. T., West, J. D., & Wiseman, M. A. (2008). The Eigenfactor metrics. Journal of Neuroscience, 28(45), 11433–11434. DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0003-08.2008.

Journal metric · Open Journal Factor (OJF)

OJF divides citations received in one year to a journal's publications in the two preceding years by its citable items in those years. Citations count when they come from sources approved in the Norwegian Register. Any eligible OpenAlex journal can receive an OJF, even if it is not itself in the register. Citing sources are fixed to those approved in the Norwegian Register for the citation year, including approved books and conference sources.

Citable items are articles, reviews, data papers and software papers. Journal self-citations are included. Citations to other content, such as editorials, also count without adding to the number of citable items.

The default is filtered OJF: citable items without recorded references, and citations to those items, are left out. Raw OJF is also available. No citable items means no OJF; citable items with no citations means an OJF of zero.

OJF values are independent calculations, not official Journal Impact Factors™. Do not mix OJF and JIF values in one comparison. Comparisons are most useful within the same field and year. OJF is shown to one decimal place in the tables and three decimal places in selection downloads; percentiles use unrounded scores.

See this study for a 2023–2024 comparison between OJF and JIF.

Journal universe
For ANS and NF, only journals in the chosen universe, and citations between them, take part in the network score calculation. The selection also determines the journals compared for percentiles. Norwegian Register includes journals approved at Level 1 or 2 that year, an annually reviewed, curated list. OpenAlex includes all sources classified as journals with at least one open score, offering broader coverage. The same journal can have different ANS and NF values in different universes.
Show journals from
For OJF, 'Show journals from' selects Norwegian Register journals approved at Level 1 or 2 that year, or the broader OpenAlex journal group. It changes the journals displayed and compared for percentiles. A journal's OJF itself does not change.
Fields
The most frequent OpenAlex primary-topic broad field among the journal's articles and reviews in the preceding two years for OJF, or five years for ANS. The percentage shows its share of classified publications. Journals without a field form the Unknown group.
Preset · AIS-oriented
The default preset uses filtered ANS in the Norwegian Register universe. Field percentiles require below 90% of publications without references, at least four publication years, and 100% retained per field. Journals without percentiles are hidden. This study supports using filtered metrics and the Norwegian Register. ANS values are independent calculations, not official Article Influence Scores.
Preset · JIF-oriented
Uses filtered OJF and Norwegian Register journals. Field percentiles require below 90% of citable items without references and 100% retained per field. Journals without percentiles are hidden. OJF values are independent calculations, not official Journal Impact Factors.
Preset · Full coverage (noisy)
Uses filtered values and the OpenAlex journal group, showing journals with and without percentiles. Field percentiles require below 90% of publications without references and 100% retained per field. ANS also requires at least four publication years; OJF has no publication-years requirement. Broader coverage can include noisier scores.
Preset · AIS-oriented (select)
Uses AIS-oriented with below 80% of publications without references and retains the top 75% per field. Shows Total percentiles averaged over the selected year and two preceding years when available. An eligible Total percentile in the selected year is required. These are Opindx percentiles, not official benchmark ranks or quartiles.
Preset · Custom
Choose Custom to open Advanced settings. Changing a setting can also label the selection Custom without opening the panel. The Advanced settings link opens or closes it; choosing a named preset closes it. Reset restores the initial ANS settings while keeping the citation year.

Advanced settings

Show percentiles
Field percentiles compare eligible journals within the same OpenAlex field. Total percentiles compare the journals retained across fields. Both use the selected metric and journal group. A percentile of 90 means the score is at least as high as 90% of the comparison group; ties receive the same percentile.
Share of publications w/out references
The maximum percentage of publications without recorded OpenAlex references. Below 80% excludes journals with 80% or more missing references. No maximum also includes journals whose percentage is unavailable.
Years with publications
For ANS only: the minimum number of years with articles or reviews in the preceding five years. At least 4 years requires publications in four or five of those years. This setting is not used for OJF.
Retain top % per field
Shown when Total percentiles are selected. The share of highest-ranked journals retained from each field for Total percentiles. Journals outside this selection can still have a Field percentile. Equal scores receive the same percentile and stay together at the selection cutoff.
Hide journals without percentiles
Shows only journals with a Field percentile when Field alone is selected, or a Total percentile when Total is selected. None turns this filter off.
Average percentiles over …
The mean of annual percentiles in the selected year and two preceding calendar years, for the chosen metric and settings. A journal needs an eligible percentile in the selected year. Missing earlier percentiles are ignored, not counted as zero. Scores and counts remain those of the selected year. Available from 2024 onward.
Open access journals only
Restricts the displayed selection to journals marked open access in the OpenAlex source metadata. This describes the journal, rather than the access status of each individual publication.
Include publications without references (noisy)
Switches from filtered to raw values. OpenAlex sometimes has no recorded references for a publication. For ANS, filtered values use only articles and reviews with recorded references. For OJF, filtering excludes citable items without recorded references and citations to those items. Raw values include them. Missing references can reflect incomplete records as well as publications that have no references in reality. The website uses filtered values by default. See this study for ANS and the OJF explanation.

Journal table

Main

Table header
Shows the publication window, citation year and OpenAlex data month. When averaging is selected, it also shows the three years used for percentiles.
OpenAlex field
The most frequent primary-topic broad field among the journal's articles and reviews: two publication years for OJF, five for ANS. The percentage is its share of classified articles and reviews, including those without references. Unknown means there are no classified publications.
Publications used
For ANS: articles and reviews from the preceding five years used in the selected calculation. Filtered values include only those with recorded references.
Citable items used
For OJF: articles, reviews, data papers and software papers from the preceding two years. Filtered OJF uses only those with a recorded reference. Citations to other content can increase the citation count without adding to the citable-item count.
Citations used
Citations in the selected year used to calculate the metric. ANS counts citations from journals in the selected universe and excludes journal self-citations. OJF counts citations from Norwegian Register sources and includes journal self-citations and citations to other journal content, such as editorials.
Pubs. w/out refs.
The percentage without a recorded OpenAlex reference in the publication window: five years of articles and reviews for ANS, or two years of citable items for OJF. Missing references can reflect incomplete records or publications with no references in reality. A dash means the percentage is unavailable. Calculated as 100 × (Raw − Filtered publications) ÷ Raw publications.
ANS · Article Network Score
Influence per article, with citations weighted by the influence of the citing journals. See Article Network Score.
OJF · Open Journal Factor
Citations from Norwegian Register sources per citable item in the preceding two years. Shown to one decimal place. See Open Journal Factor.
Percentiles · Field
The selected metric's percentile within its OpenAlex field. These are averages when averaging is selected; see Advanced settings. They are Opindx percentiles, not official benchmark ranks.
Percentiles · Total
The selected metric's percentile among all retained journals across fields. These are averages when averaging is selected; see Advanced settings. They are Opindx percentiles, not official benchmark ranks.
General
A dash means that a value is unavailable; a numeric zero is a defined value. Use × beside a column header to hide it in the main table. Journal title always remains visible. Restore columns brings back hidden columns selected in the current percentile and Add columns settings. Hiding columns also removes them from selection downloads. It keeps the preset, sort order, filters, percentile calculation and journal popup unchanged. These display choices last for this visit. Reset or reloading the page restores the default columns.

Add columns

NF · Network Factor
The journal's share of citation-network influence in the selected universe, following the citation-network method of Bergstrom, West and Wiseman (2008). Citations are weighted by the influence of the citing journal. NF sums to 100 across that universe; larger journals can receive a larger share. It uses the same five-year publication window as ANS and excludes journal self-citations. The Network Factor follows an Eigenfactor™-like methodology. Available when ANS is selected; missing ANS does not imply missing NF.

Reference
Bergstrom, C. T., West, J. D., & Wiseman, M. A. (2008). The Eigenfactor metrics. Journal of Neuroscience, 28(45), 11433–11434. DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0003-08.2008.

Publication years
The number of years in the five years preceding the citation year in which at least one publication is recorded for the journal.
Publications recorded
All recorded articles and reviews in the preceding five years for ANS, including those not used in its scores. For OJF, Citable items recorded counts all citable items in the preceding two years.
Citable items recorded
For OJF: all recorded articles, reviews, data papers and software papers in the preceding two years, including those not used in filtered OJF.
Citations recorded
For ANS: recorded citations from all available sources to the five-year publications, excluding journal self-citations, including citations not used in the selected score. For OJF: raw citations from Norwegian Register sources to the two-year publications, including journal self-citations.
OpenAlex ID
The OpenAlex journal ID, linked to its dedicated journal page.
Norwegian Register ID
The journal's Norwegian Register ID(s) in the selected year, linked to their dedicated journal pages.
Norwegian Register level
The journal's Norwegian Register level in that year. A dash means no approved level (1 or 2) is available in our data. See the level explanation.
ISSNs
All available journal ISSNs, separated by spaces.

Details

OpenAlex details
A link to the journal's dedicated OpenAlex page, with its OpenAlex data and ID.
Norwegian Register details
A link to the journal's dedicated Norwegian Register page, with its register data and ID.
Open access journal
Whether the journal is recorded as open access in the OpenAlex database.
Publications recorded / used
For ANS, Recorded counts all articles and reviews in the preceding five years; Used counts those included in the selected calculation. For OJF, Citable items recorded / used applies the same distinction to citable items in the preceding two years.
Citations recorded / used
Used counts citations entering the selected calculation. For ANS, Recorded also includes citations from outside the chosen universe; both counts exclude journal self-citations. For OJF, Recorded is the raw citation count from Norwegian Register sources; both counts include journal self-citations.
Publication years
For ANS: the number of years with articles or reviews in the preceding five years. Not used for OJF.
Publications w/out references
The percentage without a recorded OpenAlex reference in the publication window: five years of articles and reviews for ANS, or two years of citable items for OJF. Missing references can reflect incomplete records or publications with no references in reality. A dash means the percentage is unavailable. Calculated as 100 × (Raw − Filtered publications) ÷ Raw publications.

Fields covered

OpenAlex primary fields
The three most frequent OpenAlex primary-topic broad fields among the journal's articles and reviews in the shown publication years: two years for OJF, five for ANS. Percentages exclude unclassified publications. Other fields combines the remaining fields.
Norwegian Register field
The journal's assigned field in the selected citation year. The Norwegian Register assigns one field to each journal, reviewed annually. This is a journal classification, not a publication percentage.

Citable items used

Data versions

Score year
The citation year. OJF uses publications from the preceding two years; ANS uses five.
Status
A live year follows the newest OpenAlex snapshot and can still change. A frozen year always comes from the same, shown OpenAlex snapshot and no longer changes, so it can be cited safely. Twelve months after the end of the score year its status changes from live to frozen. For example, 2025 is updated live with every OpenAlex snapshot until the end of 2026. At that point its status and metrics are frozen.
Data run
The archived release supplying that year's data. The link opens its files.
OpenAlex snapshot
The month of the official OpenAlex data snapshot used to calculate the metrics.
Norwegian Register snapshot
The date of the official Norwegian Register data used. Journal membership, fields and levels refer to the selected citation year.
